Our story

From a family kitchen to your neighbourhood favourite

Tasty Corner started in 2015 as a tiny family kitchen with one oven and a handful of recipes passed down from our grandmother.

Today we serve hundreds of guests every week, but the idea is the same: use fresh ingredients, cook every dish to order, and make everyone feel at home.
